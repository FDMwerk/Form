## Evidence and limitations

Earlier development checks covered original STL/CAD/mesh fixtures, dimensions, imports, original colors and textures, native file/bridge/preferences policies, localization structure, measurements, transforms and UI interactions. Standalone math tests cover known 1 cm³ volume, transformed/projected areas, malformed/open topology, cache invalidation and print-estimate monotonicity. Display tests cover material/clipping restoration, helper disposal and camera projections; section tests cover actual triangle-plane contours, coplanar boundaries, flips, visibility and transforms. Refer to [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md) and the final test log for the exact last integrated run; this audit did not rerun or upgrade historical results into new hardware claims.

A universal binary has not by itself established testing on a physical Intel Mac, macOS 14 or every MacBook. Native-speaker review of all translations, long-session/large-input performance, full keyboard/VoiceOver operation, contrast, RTL and narrow-window behavior remain explicit checks for the final candidate. Large synchronous tessellation and section recomputation on a changed plane can pause a complex scene.

Measurements and volumes use tessellated display geometry, not certified CAD metrology. Open/inconsistently wound meshes cannot supply a reliable closed volume. Closed-edge checks do not detect self-intersections; component volumes are additive and overlaps are not removed. Print-time ranges are engineering heuristics, not calibrated slicer results. Sections fill verified closed-mesh cuts with holes preserved, fall back to contours for open/ambiguous meshes and provide no geometry export; docking creates no persistent constraint; transforms are session-only. Do not advertise “all CAD formats,” a parametric CAD modeller or guaranteed print times.
