Local processing

Model files are read locally. Form does not automatically upload models, collect analytics or store Bambu credentials. Imports create local session copies in the app's temporary storage. Preferences are stored locally. Sharing files or screenshots only occurs when you choose a destination in Apple's share sheet. The recipient app's privacy terms apply. This prototype does not slice or start prints.
